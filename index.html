    04 / CLIMATE & MONSOON LAB

    How the Southwest Monsoon actually happens

    Step through the mechanism stage by stage — from summer land heating to the two monsoon branches splitting at the Western Ghats and the Bay of Bengal.

    Arabian Sea branch — strikes the Western Ghats first, causing heavy orographic rainfall on the windward (western) slope and a rain-shadow on the Deccan Plateau to the east.
    Bay of Bengal branch — moves up towards Bengal & Northeast India, is deflected west by the Himalayan wall, and carries rain along the Gangetic plain — heaviest near the Meghalaya hills (Mawsynram/Cherrapunji).
