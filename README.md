## 🧭 Overview & Mission

**BHARAT ATLAS MASTER** is an exhaustive, pedagogical, offline-first web platform and visual knowledge engine designed to cover the entire **World and Indian Geography** curriculum for the UPSC Civil Services Examination and State Public Service Commissions.

Grounding every topic in the canonical standard literature:
* **Prof. Majid Husain:** *Geography of India* & *World Geography* (McGraw Hill / Rawat)
* **NCERT Textbooks:** Class 11 (*Fundamentals of Physical Geography* & *India: Physical Environment*), Class 12 (*Fundamentals of Human Geography* & *India: People and Economy*)
* **Goh Cheng Leong (G.C. Leong):** *Certificate Physical and Human Geography* (Oxford)
* **Dr. D.R. Khullar:** *India: A Comprehensive Geography* (Kalyani)
* **Dr. Savindra Singh:** *Physical Geography* (Prayag Pustak Bhawan)

### 2. 🌍 Salient Features of World's Physical Geography (`worldgeo`)
* **🌋 Geomorphology:**
  * *Origin & Evolution of Earth:* Big Bang Theory, Nebular Hypothesis, Accretion, Core Differentiation, Volcanic Outgassing, and the Great Oxidation Event.
  * *Interior of the Earth:* Concentric layers (Crust, Mantle, Asthenosphere, Outer Core, Inner Core), Seismic wave mechanics (P-waves vs S-waves), and Discontinuities (Conrad, Mohorovičić, Repetti, Gutenberg, Lehmann).
  * *Distribution of Continents & Oceans:* Continental Drift Theory (Alfred Wegener, Pangaea, Panthalassa, Paleomagnetic evidence, Polar wandering).
  * *Plate Tectonic Theory:* Harry Hess seafloor spreading, 7 Major Plates, Minor Plates, Divergent/Convergent (O-O, O-C, C-C)/Transform boundaries, Ring of Fire. Note on why C-C convergence (Himalayas) produces fold mountains without volcanism.
  * *Distribution of Earthquakes & Volcanoes:* Circum-Pacific, Alpine-Himalayan, Mid-Atlantic belts, and Intraplate Hotspots (Hawaii, Réunion).
  * *Rocks & Rock Cycle:* Igneous (Intrusive Granite/Gabbro vs Extrusive Basalt), Sedimentary (Mechanically, Organically, Chemically formed), Metamorphic (Foliated vs Non-foliated), and the Rock Cycle.
  * *Geomorphic Processes:* Endogenic (Diastrophism: Orogenic/folding vs Epeirogenic/uplift; Sudden: Volcanism & Quakes) vs Exogenic (Weathering: physical, chemical, biological; Mass Wasting; Erosion & Deposition).
  * *Landforms & their Evolution:* Fluvial, Glacial, Karst, Arid/Aeolian (Barchans, Yardangs, Zeugen), and Coastal landforms.
  * *Geological Time Scale:* Eons, Eras, Periods, and Epochs from Precambrian to Quaternary (Pleistocene, Holocene, Anthropocene).
* **🌊 Oceanography:**
  * *Hydrological Cycle:* Global reservoir breakdown (97.5% saline, 2.5% freshwater), water fluxes, and residence times.
  * *Seafloor Spreading:* Magnetic zebra-striping, oceanic crust age gradient, and sediment thickness proofs.
  * *Ocean Floor Configuration:* Continental Shelf, Continental Slope, Continental Rise, Abyssal Plains, Deep Oceanic Trenches (Mariana, Java), Mid-Ocean Ridges, Guyots, Seamounts, Submarine Canyons.
  * *Temperature & Salinity of Oceans:* Vertical layers (Epipelagic, Thermocline, Deep cold zone), horizontal gradients, Halocline, and hypersaline bodies (Lake Van 330‰, Dead Sea 238‰, Great Salt Lake 220‰, Red Sea 41‰).
  * *Movement of Oceans:* Wave orbital kinematics; Tides (Spring tides at syzygy vs Neap tides at quadrature; Bay of Fundy & Gulf of Khambhat); Ocean Currents (Gyres, Coriolis deflection, Warm currents like Gulf Stream/Kuroshio vs Cold currents like Canary/Benguela/Peru, desertification of western coasts, and global fishing banks at current meeting zones).
* **⛅ Climatology:**
  * *Earth’s Atmosphere:* Composition (N2 78%, O2 21%, Ar 0.93%, CO2 0.04%, aerosols), vertical layers (Troposphere, Stratosphere, Mesosphere, Thermosphere/Ionosphere, Exosphere), Normal Lapse Rate (-6.5°C/km), and tropopause height variation (18 km at equator vs 8 km at poles).
  * *Solar Radiation, Heat Budget & Temperature:* Insolation factors, Albedo table, 100-Unit Heat Budget, and Temperature Inversion types (Radiation, Valley, Advection).
  * *Atmospheric Circulation & Weather Systems:* Planetary pressure belts (Equatorial Low, Subtropical Highs, Subpolar Lows, Polar Highs), Tri-cellular model (Hadley, Ferrel, Polar cells), Planetary winds (Trades, Westerlies, Polar Easterlies), Jet Streams, Air Masses, and Fronts.
  * *World Climate (Köppen Classification):* Af Rainforest, Am Monsoon, Aw Savanna, BWh Hot Desert, Cs Mediterranean (dry summer, wet winter), Cfa Humid Subtropical, Cfb Marine West Coast, Dfb Continental, ET Tundra, EF Ice Cap.
* **🌱 Soil Geography:**
  * *Soil Contents & Profile:* 45% minerals, 5% organic matter, 25% water, 25% air; Horizons O, A, E, B, C, R; Solum (A+E+B).
  * *Process of Soil Formation:* Humification, Mineralization, Eluviation, Illuviation, Laterization (silica leached), Podzolization (iron/aluminium leached), Calcification, Salinization.
  * *Soil Forming Factors:* Hans Jenny equation $S = f(Cl, O, R, P, T)$ (Active vs Passive factors).
  * *Types of Soils:* USDA 12 Orders (Inceptisols #1 in India ~39%, Entisols, Alfisols, Vertisols, Mollisols, Oxisols, Aridisols) and Global Zonal Soils (Chernozem, Podzol, Laterite, Terra Rossa).
  * *Soil Erosion & Conservation:* Splash, Sheet (most insidious), Rill, Gully erosion; Agronomic, mechanical, and biological conservation.

### 3. 🏭 Distribution of Key Natural Resources & Industrial Location (`resources`)
* **Natural Resources (Global & South Asia):**
  * *Types of Resources:* By Origin (Biotic vs Abiotic), Ownership (Individual, Community, National [12 nm], International [>200 nm UNCLOS/ISA]), Exhaustibility (Renewable vs Non-renewable), Status of Development (Potential, Developed, Stock, Reserves).
  * *Land Resources:* Nine-fold land use classification, Net Sown Area (~43% in India), Gross Cropped Area, Land degradation drivers (over-irrigation in Punjab/Haryana causing Usar/Reh, overgrazing in Gujarat/MP, mining scars in Chota Nagpur), and reclamation using Gypsum.
  * *Forest Resources:* Global biomes (Amazon Rainforests, Monsoon Deciduous, Boreal/Taiga pure softwood stands, Temperate Grasslands); National Forest Policy 1988 (33% national target), Social Forestry, Joint Forest Management (JFM), REDD+.
  * *Water Resources:* Global vs Indian water balance (India has 18% population, 4% water); Falkenmark Water Stress indicator (<1,700 m³ stress, <1,000 m³ scarcity); Integrated Water Resources Management (IWRM), Watershed development (Sukhomajri, Hiware Bazar, Arvari), Jal Jeevan Mission, Atal Bhujal Yojana, and National River Interlinking (Ken-Betwa link).
  * *Agricultural Resources:* Farming types (Primitive subsistence/Jhum/Milpa/Ladang, Intensive subsistence wet paddy, Commercial grain in Prairies/Steppes, Plantations, Mixed farming, Dairy); Cropping patterns (Kharif, Rabi, Zaid); Food Security architecture (NFSA 2013, PDS, buffer stocks, MSP by CCEA, Millets / Shree Anna).
  * *Mineral & Energy Resources:* Ferrous (Hematite & Magnetite iron ore, Manganese, Chromite) vs Non-Ferrous (Bauxite, Copper, Lead, Zinc); Major Indian mineral belts (Chota Nagpur 'Ruhr of India', South-Western belt, North-Western belt); Conventional Energy (Coal ranks Anthracite/Bituminous/Lignite/Peat, Gondwana 98% vs Tertiary coal, Petroleum basins Mumbai High/Barmer/KG-D6, Uranium at Jaduguda/Tummalapalle, Thorium in Kerala monazite beach sands); Clean energy transition (Panchamrit 500 GW targets, ISA, National Green Hydrogen Mission).
* **Industrial Location Factors:**
  * *Classification of Industries:* Raw material inputs (Agro, Mineral, Chemical, Forest, Animal), Scale (MSME 2020 composite criteria), Ownership (Public, Private, Joint, Cooperative).
  * *Alfred Weber's Least Cost Theory:* Material Index $MI = \frac{\text{Weight of localized raw materials}}{\text{Weight of finished product}}$. Weight-losing raw materials ($MI > 1$) pull factories to raw material sites (Iron & Steel, Sugar); Pure raw materials ($MI = 1$) allow market or intermediate locations (Cotton textiles); Isodapanes and Critical Isodapane.
  * *Ten Determinants of Location:* Raw Materials, Energy/Power, Market, Labour, Transport, Water, Land, Capital, Government Grants/SEZs, Communication.
  * *Distribution of Major Industries:*
    * *Iron & Steel:* Global belts (US Rust Belt, Ruhr Valley Germany, Anshan-Wuhan China, Nippon Steel Japan) & Indian plants (Tata Steel Jamshedpur, Bhilai, Rourkela, Durgapur, Bokaro, IISCO, Visakhapatnam shore-based plant, JSW Vijayanagar, Salem).
    * *IT & Electronics:* Silicon Valley California, Route 128 Boston, Hsinchu Taiwan, Bengaluru ('Silicon Valley of India'), Hyderabad, Pune, Gurugram, Noida.
    * *Cotton Textiles:* Historical concentration in humid coastal sites (Lancashire, Mumbai, Ahmedabad); subsequent spatial decentralization with artificial humidifiers to powerloom clusters (Surat, Bhiwandi, Tirupur, Coimbatore).
  * *Agglomeration Economies & Footloose Industries:* Shared infrastructure, ancillary linkages, industrial inertia, and footloose industries (high value-to-weight ratio, component-based, zero weight loss, e.g. microchips, software, diamond cutting).

### 4. ⚡ Important Geophysical Phenomena & Critical Environmental Shifts (`geophysical`)
* **⚡ Earthquakes:** Elastic Rebound Theory, Focus vs Epicentre, P-waves, S-waves, Rayleigh and Love surface waves, P-wave and S-wave Shadow Zones (S-wave shadow zone 103° to 180°), Types (Tectonic, Volcanic, Collapse, Explosion, Reservoir-Induced Seismicity Koyna 1967), Richter magnitude (logarithmic 31.6x energy) vs Modified Mercalli intensity (I-XII), Soil Liquefaction.
* **🌊 Tsunamis:** Submarine megathrust earthquakes, submarine landslides, caldera explosions; Deep ocean propagation (800 km/h, <1 m height) vs Coastal Shoaling effect (30-50 km/h, 10-30 m height); Inundation and backwash; DART sensor buoys, INCOIS Early Warning Centre (Hyderabad), mangrove bio-shields (Pichavaram), and IOWave mock drills.
* **🌋 Volcanoes:** Magma genesis (decompression melting, flux melting, mantle plumes); Shield volcanoes (Mauna Loa fluid basalt), Composite stratovolcanoes (Mt. Fuji andesitic explosive), Calderas (Yellowstone, Krakatoa), Flood basalts (Deccan Traps); Intrusive plutonic landforms (Batholiths, Laccoliths, Lapoliths, Phacoliths, Sills concordant, Dykes discordant); Extrusive landforms; Barren Island (India's only active volcano) and Narcondam (dormant).
* **🌀 Cyclones:** Tropical Cyclones genesis criteria (SST >27°C, Coriolis force $\ne 0$ i.e. >5° lat, low vertical wind shear, eye, eye wall, rainbands); Bay of Bengal vs Arabian Sea storm frequency asymmetry and recent warming trends; Anti-cyclones (high pressure, subsiding air, clear skies); Extra-Tropical Cyclones (Polar Front Theory, cold and warm fronts, Western Disturbances bringing vital winter Rabi rainfall to NW India).
* **❄️ Changes in Critical Geographical Features & Environmental Impacts:**
  * *Melting Ice Sheets & Glaciers:* Shrinking Arctic sea ice, Antarctic ice shelf calving (Larsen C, Thwaites 'Doomsday' glacier), Himalayan 'Third Pole' glacier retreat and GLOFs (South Lhonak 2023, Chamoli 2021).
  * *Desiccation of Inland Water Bodies:* Aral Sea desiccation into the Aralkum Desert, Lake Chad 90% shrinkage, Dead Sea level decline (>1 m/yr).
  * *Desertification:* Sahel southward encroachment (Great Green Wall), Thar desert eastward drift.
  * *Impacts:* Sea level rise ($3.7\text{ mm/yr}$), coastal inundation (Sundarbans), saltwater intrusion, coral bleaching from marine heatwaves (Great Barrier Reef, Lakshadweep), upslope treeline migrations, and faunal range contractions.

### 5. 🏛️ Physical, Human & Economic Geography of India
* **Physiography of India:** Himalayas (Trans-Himalayas, Greater, Lesser, Shiwalik, Purvanchal), Indo-Gangetic Plains (Bhabar, Terai, Bhangar, Khadar), Peninsular Plateau (Central Highlands, Deccan Plateau, Western & Eastern Ghats), Coastal Plains, Thar Desert, Andaman & Nicobar and Lakshadweep islands.
* **Drainage Systems:** Antecedent Himalayan rivers (Indus, Ganga, Brahmaputra) vs Superimposed Peninsular rivers (Godavari, Krishna, Cauvery, Narmada, Tapi, Mahanadi).
* **Climate of India:** 8-stage monsoon physics, Somali Jet, ITCZ migration, Tropical Easterly Jet, Western Disturbances, El Niño, La Niña, Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD), and Madden-Julian Oscillation (MJO).
* **Soils in India:** ICAR 8 groups: Alluvial, Black (Regur/Vertisols), Red & Yellow, Laterite, Arid, Saline-Alkaline (Usar), Peaty, Forest soils.
* **Natural Vegetation:** 5 major forest types, FSI India State of Forest Report (ISFR) data, Tiger Reserves, and Biosphere Reserves.
* **Human Geography & Demography:** Population size (1.21 B in 2011, >1.43 B in 2023), density (382 / km²), growth trends, 1921 'Year of the Great Divide', Demographic Transition Model stages, Sex ratio (943), Child sex ratio (919), Literacy (73.0%), Demographic Dividend.
* **Urbanization:** Census definition (Statutory vs Census towns), 31.16% urban share, million-plus cities (53), megacities (Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata), urban heat islands, loss of blue-green infrastructure, Smart Cities Mission, and AMRUT.
* **Census History:** 1872 non-synchronous (Mayo) to 1881 synchronous (Ripon), Census Act 1948, RG&CCI, SECC 2011, and Upcoming Digital Census.
* **Economic Geography:** Agriculture (Kharif, Rabi, Zaid, Green Revolution), Minerals, Energy, Industrial regions (Mumbai-Pune, Hugli, Bengaluru-Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Chota Nagpur), and Infrastructure (Bharatmala, Golden Quadrilateral, DFCs, National Waterways NW-1/2/3, Sagarmala, UDAN).
